Recurring Task Tracking & Escalation Automation

Assignment prototype developed for the AI Operations / Founder's Office assignment at Allo Innoware.

The Business Problem

Recurring operational tasks were assigned to multiple employees. Tasks repeated at different intervals — daily, every 7 days, every 15 days, and every 30 days.

Manual task tracking creates risks such as:

⚠️ Missed deadlines
📋 Duplicate updates
📎 Incomplete proof submission
👁️ Lack of management visibility
⏱️ Delayed escalation
🤚 Manual follow-up dependency

My Solution

Designed and implemented a Google Sheets and Google Apps Script-based recurring task management prototype.

Practical Employee Experience

Portal Tasks

  1. Employee enters the Ticket ID in the Work Queue.
  2. Adds completion notes.
  3. Ticks Mark Complete.
  4. The script records completion.

Daily WhatsApp Tasks

  1. Employee shares an operational update in the WhatsApp group.
  2. Captures screenshot proof.
  3. Uploads proof to Google Drive.
  4. Pastes the Drive link into the Work Queue.
  5. Adds completion notes.
  6. Ticks Mark Complete.

The system records the completion and evidence link. It does not automatically read or verify WhatsApp messages.

Working days and timings are configurable in the Settings sheet.
